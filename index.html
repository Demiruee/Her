I'm sorry. I know there are things I could have handled better, and I know that some of my actions have hurt you. I never wanted you to feel that you weren't important to me, that spending time with you was becoming a burden, or that I was slowly getting tired of us. I hope you can understand that there's a side of me I've been struggling to explain, and I wish you could see where I'm coming from, too.

I wasn't trying to point out that your demands were too much or that you were asking for more than I could give. What I wanted was for you to understand why I sometimes do things the way I do. I have this tendency to solve my own problems without asking for help because, somehow, asking someone else to help me makes me feel incompetent, like I'm incapable of handling my own struggles. I know that mindset isn't always healthy, and I know it sometimes leads to misunderstandings between us, but it's something I've been trying to work through. I want to learn how to let someone in without feeling like I've failed myself. And I want that someone to be you. There are moments when I wish I could just ask for your opinion, hear your thoughts, get your guidance, or even let you help me make decisions when I can no longer trust my own judgment. I don't always know how to ask for those things, but that doesn't mean I don't need you.

There's also something personal that I haven't fully learned how to deal with. Some things I've done in the past still haunt me, and I deeply regret them. Even something as simple as walking you through CSU when you're going home can sometimes be difficult because certain places bring back memories of the things I wish I had done differently. I understand how it might look from your perspective, and I understand why you would feel hurt or question my actions. But please know that it has nothing to do with not wanting to be with you. I'm struggling with the weight of my own past, and sometimes it affects things I never intended it to affect. I wish I could explain all of this without making it seem like I'm making excuses for myself.

I also hope you understand that I'm not shutting myself off from you. My door is still open. I'm just not always standing at the entrance, explaining everything that's happening inside me. Sometimes, I'm already inside, quietly waiting and hoping that you'll come in, sit beside me, hold me, and understand that I don't always need to explain what's wrong before I can be comforted. I know I have to learn to communicate better, and I'm not expecting you to read my mind. But I hope you understand that expressing things like these takes time for me. They're deeply personal and sensitive, and I'm not someone who easily shares what's going on inside. It doesn't mean I don't trust you or that I don't want you to know. Sometimes, I just don't know where to begin.

Honestly, it hurts when I feel like everything I've done is being misunderstood, especially when I know the intentions behind those actions. It hurts even more when it feels like you've already built up an idea of me being someone who doesn't want to make an effort for you. You know I wouldn't intentionally treat you that way. I know neither of us is perfect, and I don't want us to keep looking for who's wrong between the two of us. I don't think either of us intended to hurt the other. I think we simply failed to understand each other's sides, and I wish we could work through that without making each other feel like the problem.

I'm also sorry for not making enough time for you, for the moments I've spent less time with you and chosen to play games instead. I hope you understand that gaming has become one of my little escapes whenever everything feels too heavy. It's where I temporarily put down the things I can't seem to carry anymore. It's never because your presence isn't enough or because I'd rather spend my time doing something else than being with you. Sometimes, I just need somewhere to let my mind rest. But I understand that even if that's how I cope, it doesn't mean I should overlook the time and attention you need from me. I'm sorry for the times my struggles have made me distant, less present, or unable to give you the affection and attention you deserve. I'm still figuring things out, and honestly, I don't always know how to balance everything, but I want to do better.

And about what you said, that maybe we shouldn't go further because loving me has become draining, I won't pretend that it didn't hurt. It did. Because the exhaustion I've been feeling isn't coming from loving you or being with you. I'm struggling with myself, with my regrets, with things I don't always know how to express. Hearing that made me feel like you were ready to let go of us because of something that I haven't even properly explained. Please don't make a decision about us while we're both overwhelmed and hurting. I don't want one difficult moment or one misunderstanding to become the reason we give up on everything we've built. I'm not asking you to ignore your feelings, and I'm not asking you to stay if you're genuinely unhappy. I just hope you'll try to understand mine, too, before deciding that we're better off without each other.

I know I still have a lot to work on. I know loving someone also means learning how to communicate, how to make time, how to be present, and how to understand things beyond my own perspective. I'm sorry for the times I fell short. I'm sorry for making you question your place in my life. And I'm sorry that sometimes, the things I struggle to say end up hurting you more than the things I actually say.

I just want you to know that I understand your side, even when I don't necessarily agree with everything. I hear you, and I don't want you to feel that your feelings are any less important than mine. I only wish you'd give my side the same space to be understood. I don't want us to keep hurting each other over things we could have talked through if only we had been a little more patient with one another.

I love you. Not just when things are easy between us, not just when I have everything figured out, and not just when I can give you all the time and attention you deserve. I love you even in the middle of all the things I'm still trying to understand about myself. I'm not struggling with the thought of having you in my future. I'm struggling with parts of myself that I haven't figured out how to leave behind. And I don't want those parts to keep getting in the way of something I genuinely want to keep.

I still want you. I still want us. And despite everything, I still want to figure things out with you, not against you.
